Your privacy matters

Introduction

Lumi ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This page explains what information the app may access, how it is used, and the choices you have. By tapping Agree & Continue below, you confirm that you have read and understood this policy and consent to continue using Lumi under these terms.

We designed Lumi to work with as little data as possible. Your privacy is not an afterthought — it is a core part of how the app is built.

Information We Access

Lumi does not require an account and does not collect personal identifiers. Depending on the features you use, the app may access the following categories of data, strictly limited to what is needed for the app to function on your device:

  • App preferences & settings — stored locally on your device to remember your choices.
  • Device information — basic technical details (such as device model and operating system version) used solely to ensure compatibility and to deliver the correct version of the app.
  • Anonymous technical diagnostics — crash logs that contain no personal information, used only to fix defects and improve stability.

We do not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera, health data, or any other sensitive data unless a specific feature you actively enable requires it, and in that case you will be asked for explicit permission first.

How Your Data Is Used

Any information accessed by Lumi is used only on your device to provide and improve the app experience you asked for. Specifically:

  • To remember your in-app settings and preferences locally.
  • To keep the app stable, secure, and compatible with your device.
  • To respond to support requests that you voluntarily send to us by email.

Your data is never used for profiling, advertising, marketing, analytics, or research purposes. We do not build user profiles, and we do not track your behavior across apps or websites.

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with third parties. Lumi does not embed third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or data brokers. Information accessed by the app stays on your device and is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, except in the very limited circumstances described below:

  • Legal requirements — if required by applicable law, we may disclose information only to the extent strictly mandated and permitted by law, after a lawful request.
  • Service providers — none. Lumi does not use external processors for your personal data.

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no data for us to share. You remain in full control.

Storage, Retention & Security

Settings and preferences are stored locally on your device. They remain only for as long as you keep them or keep the app installed. Uninstalling Lumi removes all locally stored data.

We use platform-standard security protections, including secure storage and encrypted system channels, to protect any data the app handles. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights & Choices

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Access & review — all data is on your device and visible through the app's settings.
  • Delete — clear app data from your device settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
  • Withdraw consent — stop using the app at any time. Since no personal data leaves your device, withdrawing consent simply means uninstalling Lumi.
  • Opt out — there is nothing to opt out of, because no data is collected, profiled, or shared.

Children's Privacy

Lumi does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region). If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements to Lumi or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will present the updated policy within the app before you continue using it. Continued use of Lumi after an update means you accept the revised policy.
