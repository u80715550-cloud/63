Overview

is designed to work for you without collecting information about you. This policy applies to the mobile application and describes the limited data that touches the app and the protections applied to it.

By tapping Agree & Continue, you confirm that you have read and understood this policy. If you do not agree, simply close the app and no further data processing will take place.

What We Collect

  • App content you create. Items, preferences, and settings you create inside are stored locally on your device and remain under your control.
  • Technical essentials. A device identifier derived on-device is used solely to check whether an updated version of this policy exists. It is not linked to your identity and is never stored alongside your content.
  • Nothing else. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, calendar, or any other personal profile data.

What We Never Do

  • No analytics or profiling. Your usage is never measured, scored, segmented, or reconstructed into behavioral profiles.
  • No third-party sharing. Your data is not sold, rented, licensed, or disclosed to advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party.
  • No cross-app tracking. We do not follow you across other apps or websites, and we do not request permission to do so.
  • No advertising SDKs. The app contains no ad networks and no marketing trackers of any kind.

How Data Is Used

The only outbound network request the app makes is a check for an updated privacy policy, sent to a serverless endpoint operated on our behalf. That request carries the app identifier and a locally computed device fingerprint, and its only purpose is to decide whether this page should redirect to a newer policy.

All other processing — rendering the interface, storing your content, remembering preferences — happens entirely on your device. No server ever receives a copy of your content.

Security & Retention

Data at rest is protected by the operating system's sandbox and device encryption. Transport for the policy check uses HTTPS with certificate validation. We keep no server-side copy of your content, so there is nothing to retain, export, or breach.

Deleting the app removes all locally stored data associated with . There is no separate account to delete.

  • Objection. You may stop using the app at any time without any loss beyond the local content itself.
